- When tasked with locating and moving objects within an environment, it is effective to systematically search probable storage spaces first, such as drawers or closets, before exploring less traditional storage locations. Breaking down the broader task into a sequence of smaller actions—locate, retrieve, and place—enables task completion with efficiency. Additionally, when carrying objects, it's important to remember that carrying capacity may be limited to one object, necessitating trips if more than one item needs to be moved.
